# Pick Your Pixel

Turn any picture into glowing particles, outlines, pixels, halftone dots, ASCII text, mosaic tiles, lines or emoji. Pick an animation, choose your colors, and save the result as a PNG or a video.

Everything runs in your browser. Your pictures are never uploaded anywhere.

## What it does
- **8 styles:** Particles, Edges, Pixels, Halftone, ASCII, Mosaic, Lines, Emoji
- **10 animations:** Assemble, Float, Wave, Swirl, Pulse, Burst, Rain, Glitch, Draw-in, Still
- **12 color palettes** plus a custom two-color one, and four ways for color to follow the picture
- **Particle shapes:** square, circle, diamond, star
- **Presets:** Cyberpunk, Gold statue, Matrix rain, Ice crystal, Sunset pop, Retro tiles, Fire
- **Text mode:** type a word and turn it into particles
- **Smart cutout** for removing busy backgrounds
- **Compare** your result with the original picture
- **Save PNG** in square, post and phone-wallpaper sizes, or **record a 6-second video**
- **Look codes:** share your exact settings with a friend as a short code
- Click or tap the picture to blast the particles apart

## How to use it
Open the live page, drop in a picture, and play with the controls.
Pictures with clean, high-contrast subjects (line art, logos, portraits) look best.

## Run it yourself
Download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser. No install needed.
